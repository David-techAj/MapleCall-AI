FAQ

Common questions

Can I switch plans later?

Yes — upgrade or downgrade anytime from your dashboard. Changes take effect at the next billing cycle.

Do unused calls roll over?

No — call credits reset each billing cycle. If you're regularly hitting your limit, we'll recommend upgrading.

How fast is onboarding?

Most agents go live within 24 hours. We handle setup personally — no technical knowledge required.

Is it CRTC compliant?

100%. DNCL integration, legal calling windows, mandatory opt-out, and call identification are all built in.

What languages are supported?

Pro and Team plans include English, French, Mandarin (普通話), and Cantonese (廣東話) — covering most Canadian real estate markets.

What support do I get?

Starter gets email support. Pro adds priority chat. Team includes a dedicated account manager and SLA guarantee.

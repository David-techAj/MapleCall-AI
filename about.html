About MapleCall AI

🍁 Built in Toronto, Canada

Our Mission

MapleCall AI exists to give Canadian real estate agents an unfair advantage. Every lead deserves a call within 60 seconds — in the right language, at the right time, fully compliant with Canadian law. We built the tool that makes that possible.

Why We Built This

Canadian real estate agents lose deals every day because leads go cold before they can call back. At the same time, the Canadian market is uniquely multilingual — English, French, Mandarin, and Cantonese are all critical in cities like Toronto, Vancouver, and Montreal.

We built MapleCall AI to solve both problems: instant automated follow-up that speaks your clients' language, and never misses a call.

CRTC Compliance First

We took CRTC compliance seriously from day one. Integrating with Canada's National Do Not Call List, enforcing legal calling windows, and handling consent management isn't an afterthought — it's built into the platform's core.

FAQ

Common questions

Can I switch plans later?

Yes — upgrade or downgrade anytime from your dashboard. Changes take effect at the next billing cycle with no penalty.

Do unused calls roll over?

No — call credits reset each billing cycle. If you're regularly hitting your limit, we'll proactively recommend an upgrade.

How fast is setup?

Most agents are live within 24 hours. We handle the setup with you personally — no technical knowledge required.

Is it CRTC compliant?

100%. DNCL integration, legal calling windows, mandatory opt-out, and business identification are all built in to every plan.

What languages are supported?

Pro and Team plans include English, French, Mandarin (普通話), and Cantonese (廣東話) — covering virtually every Canadian real estate market.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — every plan starts with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. Cancel anytime before the trial ends and pay nothing.

Not sure which plan fits?

Book a free 20-minute call. We'll walk through your pipeline and set up the right plan — live, in under 24 hours.

About NorthReach

🍁 Built in Toronto, Canada

Our Mission

NorthReach exists to give Canadian businesses an unfair advantage in customer acquisition. Every lead deserves a response within seconds — in the right language, at the right time, fully compliant with Canadian law. We started with real estate, and we're built to do the same for any business that can't afford to lose a lead to slow follow-up.

Why We Built This

Canadian businesses lose deals every day because leads go cold before anyone follows up. At the same time, the Canadian market is uniquely multilingual — English, French, Mandarin, and Cantonese are all critical in cities like Toronto, Vancouver, and Montreal.

We built NorthReach to solve both problems: instant automated engagement that speaks your customers' language, across voice, SMS, email, and chat — and never misses a lead.

CRTC Compliance First

We took CRTC compliance seriously from day one. Integrating with Canada's National Do Not Call List, enforcing legal calling windows, and handling consent management isn't an afterthought — it's built into the platform's core.
